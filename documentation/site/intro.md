## Main parts of VAssist

### Full chat

- [Chat and voice](/guide/chat-and-voice) for typed chat, attachments, voice conversation, message actions, and reply playback.
- [Saved chats and branches](/guide/chat-history-and-branches) for renamed chats, search, delete, and alternate replies from the same prompt.
- [Camera and screen share](/guide/camera-and-screen-share) for desktop capture that sits beside chat.

### On-page tools

- [AI toolbar](/guide/ai-toolbar) for rewrite, summarize, translate, dictation, image actions, and quick output without opening the full chat every time.

### Live companion

- [Virtual companion](/guide/virtual-companion) for the floating avatar, chat bubble, live controls, and everyday companion behavior.
- [Avatar, motions, and emotes](/guide/avatar-motions-and-emotes) for PMX or BPMX models, PMX stages, VMD or BVMD motions, and custom emote libraries.
